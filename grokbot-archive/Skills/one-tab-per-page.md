# One browser, one tab per page

## When
Before any eFleets, Google Sheet, or OneStep work.

## Rule
- All oil and OneStep bots share ONE Chrome on the shared computer. Do not open a second browser.
- At most one tab per destination: Automations `eFleets All Cars sorted`, one eFleets page, one OneStep History.
- Close duplicate sheet tabs and leftover login tabs.
- Aw Snap → blue Reload before reading or writing. Do not type into a crashed tab.
- One writer on a row. If Zachary’s cursor is on the cell, hold.
- Do not Find-type into the grid.

## After
Leave only the tabs still needed. Stay off the tab when the write is done.
